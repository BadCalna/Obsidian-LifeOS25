> [!important] ## Topic: A review of six phases of data analysis
- **ASK**
	- Gain basic knowledge or context of the problem - U can't solve a problem without knowing it
		- Some inspiring points:
			- What is the problem ur going to solve? Give it a definition
			- What are stakeholders' expectations? Must know it well
			- Make a line of Communication with stakeholders
			- See the whole picture of the problem
		- Questions that help
			- What are my stakeholders saying their problems are?
			- Now that I’ve identified the issues, how can I help the stakeholders resolve their questions?
- **PREPARE**
	- Determine the data you need to answer your question
		- Decide 
			- metrics to measure
			  需要衡量的指标
			- data to locate
			- way to protect
		- Questions that help
			- What do I need to figure out how to solve this problem?
			- What research do I need to do?
- **PROCESS**
	- Clean and organize data to make sure it's ready for use
		- You should 
			- Find incorrect data
			- Check for no extra space - for example
			- Remove repeated entries
		- Questions that help
			- What data errors or inaccuracies might get in my way of getting the best possible answer to the problem I am trying to solve?
			- How can I clean my data so the information I have is more consistent?
- **ANALYZE**
	- Use your organized data to do some analysis by performing calculations or sth
		- You may
			- Perform calculations
			- Combine data from different views
			- Create tables with your results
		- Questions that help
			- What story is my data telling me?
			- How will my data help me solve this problem?
			- Who needs my company’s product or service? What type of person is most likely to use it?
- **SHARE**
	- Summarize your results with clear and enticing visuals of your analysis using data via tools like graphs or dashboards
		- Questions that help
			- How can I make what I present to the stakeholders engaging and easy to understand?
			- What would help me understand this if I were the listener?
- **ACT**
	- Take insights from your data into action
		- Questions that help
			- How can I use the feedback I received during the share phase (step 5) to actually meet the
