> [!important] ## Topic: How data empowers decisions
- Data-driven decision making
- Data-inspired decision making
	- Explore **multiple data sources** to find out what's in **common**
> Both way mentioned above admitted that data is inherently valuable for making a decision
- Data --> Informations(giving context) --> Knowledge
- Algorithm
	- A process or a set of rules to be followed for **a** **specific** **task**
### Data does not make decisions, but it does improve them!
>[!example] ## Further
### **Data-driven decision**
---
- Using facts to guide business strategy.
- Which means: **Data is used to arrive at a decision.**
#### Limited element: Quantity and quality of readily-available data. 
- If data is sufficient and unbiased, the Decision will be far improved. Else the Decision can be very difficult to make.
#### Potential Danger:
- Over-reliance on historical data
- A tendency to ignore qualitative insights
- Potential bias
#### **A/B test**: Controlled Experiment
---
### **Data-inspired decision**
---
- One more layer of complexity
- can avoid some of the pitfalls that data-driven decisions might be prone to.
	- by introducing more thoughts such as **compare to related concepts**, **weigh in experience and feelings** ……
#### An Example: CSAT score(Abbreviation for Customer SATisfaction)
- Combine the gathered CSAT scores with data from interviewing
---
### Data triumphs and Failures
---
#### Example of Failures - 1
- Company: Coke Inc.
- Description: They develop a new Coke Formula, and tested【data gathering】. It was good-tasted. 【Conclude】So they decide replace new coke with classic coke【**no data gathering**】. 
- Analysis: They just gather subjects' opinion about new formula without asking them if it is OK to replace the classic Coke with new Coke.
#### 2
- Institution: NASA
- Description: Two team do calculate based on different unit, so the Orbiter exploded. 
- Conclusion: **Sometimes, accurate data can lead to failure.**

> [!Tips] ## Key takeaways
- **Data does not make decisions, but it does improve them.**
- **Keep a data-driven mindset and ask lots of questions.**
