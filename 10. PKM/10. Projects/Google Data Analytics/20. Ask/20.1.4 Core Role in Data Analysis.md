> [!important] ## Topic 
- **Data Analyst**: generally someone who works with SQL, spreadsheets, databases, might work as a business intelligence team creating those dashboards.
- **Data Engineer**: turn the raw data into actionable pipelines.
- **Data Scientist**: worked actually to turn it into really cool machine learning models or statistical inferences

> [!Tips] ## Key takeaways
- Try those roles all, find out what do you really exciting about.
