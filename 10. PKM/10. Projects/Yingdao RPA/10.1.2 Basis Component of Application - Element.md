> [!note] ## Usage and Feature
## Definition
---
- Element: The smallest unit that the RPA robot can operate on. ^fd44ce
## Usage
---
- Capture an element
- Capture a set of elements
	- The system automatically identifies the **maximum similarity** between the two selected elements, and based on this, captures all elements that meet the conditions.
